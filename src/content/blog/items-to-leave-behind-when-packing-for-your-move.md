Moving is a significant life event that can cause immense stress, especially if you have a plethora of other tasks in your schedule. Balancing work, family, and social life during a move might seem near impossible.

Take a deep breath and tell yourself you can do it. Taking it one step at a time and concentrating on being organized, and preparing for your move will be better than you think.

A critical aspect of moving is deciding what to pack and what not to pack. Packing everything in sight might be tempting if you're a self-proclaimed hoarder. However, there are certain belongings that you should leave behind when it comes time to pack up your things for your move. Some items pose a danger, and you may not even know it. Knowledge is power, so here are nine things you should leave behind.

## 1. Household Cleaners

Household cleaners that contain bleach can not only give off fumes that are dangerous to breathe, but they can also cause fires when combined with other chemicals. Remember that it can get very hot in the back of a moving truck in a short span, especially in the summer months. When chemicals reach intense temperatures, they have the potential to turn into dangerous gasses.

## 2 . Fertilizer and Weed Killer

Fertilizers and weed killers are flammable and shouldn't be hot, particularly for a long trip. If you plan to pack away your gardening tools for the move, don't pack fertilizer or weed killer for the back of the moving truck. It could be bad news for bears if a fertilizer or weed killer accidentally enters the moving truck.

## 3. Ammunition

Transporting ammunition in the back of a moving truck is a big no-no. If ammo gets hot, it can cause an explosion and if someone is near, it can be fatal. It is definitely not worth the risk.

If you must take ammunition with you, make sure to keep it in a cool vehicle. It’s also important that the shells stay away from the firearms for safety reasons—transport ammunition with extreme caution.

## 4. Lighter Fluid

Lighter fluid is highly flammable, and you should avoid it when packing for a move. It could combust and burn in the back of a hot moving truck. Why even risk it? If you've got any lighter fluid, give it to a friend who might use it or properly dispose of it. Please don't bring it with you.

## 5. Moving Documents

You may need to refer to your moving documents at some point during your move, but there is no way you will find them if you pack them away among dozens of boxes. If a problem arises or you disagree with your mover, it will be convenient for you to have your moving documents on hand.

## 6. Valuables

Keep your valuables out of the boxes the moving truck will transport. There's no guarantee that they won't get lost or stolen, so to be safe, carry them with you. Valuable items include expensive jewelry, cash, wills, deeds, or any other important documents. It’s best to keep these things in your own possession throughout the move.

## 7. Car Batteries

Car batteries should not be transported in the back of a moving truck since they can leak, which is a major fire hazard. Car batteries can also lead to burns on the skin if you come into contact with one that is leaking.

## 8. Flammable or Explosive Items

Do not pack items that are corrosive, flammable, or explosive. Some of these items include:

- Gasoline
- Fertilizer
- Acid
- Poison
- Lighter Fluid
- Charcoal
- Paint
- Car Battery
- Matches
- fireworks
- Nail polish
- Motor oil
- Nail Polish Remover
- Weapons
- Bleach
- Ammonia
- Aerosols
- Propane
- Kerosene
- Pool chemicals
- Chemistry sets

## 9. Perishables Foods

Don’t take perishable foods or plants with you. Imagine smelling spoiled food for half your drive. Yuck! A good rule of thumb is to empty out your refrigerator or freezer about 24 hours or more before the moving truck arrives. With the current cost of groceries, your neighbors will appreciate a food donation since you can’t take it with you anyways. Sealed packages of dry foods are acceptable.

After you empty out your freezer and refrigerator, leave the doors open to air out the inside to prevent mold or bacteria from growing and causing an unbearable odor. Oh, and we don’t think you would, but please don’t pack any pets in the moving truck, either.

Your Utah moving company will most likely provide you with a list of items not allowed for transport in the moving truck. You should avoid flammable, corrosive, or explosive things, as well as perishable items such as food and plants. You should carry valuables, important documents, and sentimental items in your own vehicle instead of transporting them in the moving truck. Pay attention to the list before packing, and contact your moving company with any specific questions. Remembering these tips, you can ensure a safe and successful move.
