Moving is one of life’s biggest stressors. Outsourcing your furniture disassembly and reassembly sounds like a great way to either reduce some of the stress of moving or add to it if you don’t trust your local movers. Some of our readers shared their experience with utilizing movers for disassembling and reassembling their furniture during a move. Find out how they felt about their decisions below.

#### Neha Gharpande

Associate at [Elite Properties NY](https://www.elitepropertiesny.com/).

## Time-Saving

When I moved houses, I hired local movers to transport my furniture. At first, I was hesitant to trust them with the assembly and disassembly of my belongings. But their positive online reviews and recommendations from friends convinced me to give them a chance.

I found that the movers I hired were professional, and experienced. They took great care of my furniture during the move. They had all the necessary gadgets required for disassembling and reassembling furniture. This gave me confidence in their ability to handle the job.

I also trusted the movers because I was pressed for time and needed to move as soon as possible. Having them handle the assembly and disassembly of my furniture saved me a lot of time and effort. This made the entire moving process smoother and less stressful.

One of the reasons why I trusted the movers was that they had liability insurance. That covered any damages caused during the move. This gave me peace of mind, knowing that I wouldn't be held liable for any accidents that might occur during the assembly or disassembly of my furniture.

Although I trusted my movers, I also had the option to assemble and disassemble my furniture myself. But I didn't have the necessary tools or experience, which would have made the process much more difficult and time-consuming. Therefore, trusting professionals was the right choice for me.

It's important to do your research and choose a reputable company that has the necessary tools, equipment, and experience. For me, the decision to trust my movers was based on their professionalism, positive reviews, and liability insurance. This ultimately made the moving process smoother and less stressful.

## Insurance Coverage

The primary reason behind my decision was the professionalism and expertise demonstrated by the moving company. They had a well-trained and experienced team, which gave me confidence in their ability to handle my furniture with care. Additionally, the company was transparent about its services, and it provided insurance coverage for any potential damages that might occur during the moving process.

I did my due diligence before hiring the movers. I researched the company, read reviews, and sought recommendations from friends and family. This extensive background check allowed me to make an informed decision and ultimately trust the movers with my furniture.

There is always an element of risk when entrusting valuable possessions to others, but in my case, the local movers proved to be reliable and efficient. They successfully assembled and disassembled my furniture without any complications or damage. I was satisfied with their service and would consider using them again in the future.
