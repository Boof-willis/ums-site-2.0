Things are getting pretty serious, eh? You're starting to think about making it a little more permanent but worry your partner may not feel the same. Maybe you'll move all their stuff into your place and surprise them. Hmmm… perhaps it's not so smart to blindside them.

If things have been going well with your partner for quite some time and you're starting to feel like living together would be a dream, then talk to them about it. Chances are, you're not the only one feeling this way. But, no matter how well things are going, it's still a big step and a step that you shouldn't make hastily. Take some time to think about it.

Moving in together is a significant investment in your relationship. If you're on the hunt for full-service movers because you're considering moving in with your partner, let's talk first. If you and your significant other are showing these five signs, you might be ready.

## You Basically Live at Each Other's Places Already

If you sleep at each other's places more than you sleep separately, then the transition won't be a big deal. In this situation, moving in together will make life easier because you won't have to go back and forth anymore. If you're sick of running home for clean clothes, you might be relieved to live together and not have to travel back and forth. It might also be a smart financial move. Why pay rent at a place you're hardly at?

## You Want to Make a More Significant Commitment to Your Partner

For some, moving in together is the perfect step between dating and marriage. You may want to commit more to your partner but aren't ready for marriage. And maybe moving in together is vital before marriage to ensure you’re compatible while living together. The idea of having your person at your home morning and night should bring a smile to your face. If so, you may be ready. If the idea incites fear, it's not the time.

## You're on the Same Page About the Future

Are you both on the same page about your relationship and where it's headed in the future? Figure this out BEFORE moving in together. A breakup is less complicated if you're not living together, so don't wait till you live in the same place to sort things out. You should clearly understand what you mean to each other, your goals as a couple, and your plans for the near and far future. Make sure nothing is up in the air.

## You Have Open Communication Regarding Finances

When you're living together your finances will naturally intertwine more than when you were dating and living separately. And living with your partner is different from living with a roommate because feelings are involved and you have a special relationship. Things may not be so clear-cut. It may be a complex subject, but discussing finances and being on the same page before moving in together is vital. Who will pay the rent and utilities? Will you split all the costs?

Feeling stable about your couple's financial situation will help you have a healthy and well-managed living situation. This will also serve you and your partner well as you progress as a couple and face hardships.

## You Aren’t Trying to Save the Relationship

Moving in Together Isn't a Band-Aid. It should NOT be the last resort to save your struggling relationship. It also should not be a significant gesture to please your partner. It probably won't turn out well if you're not all in and excited to make the next step.

If you're moving in to sort out your issues and solve them, it will only be a Band-Aid, a short-term fix that will fizzle out and reveal the problem once again. It's too big a step to make for the wrong reasons. You shouldn't move in together if your decision is motivated by the following:

- Being able to trust your partner easier
- Wanting more time together
- Needing help financially
- To solve a relationship problem
- To save your relationship
- To force your partner to commit

Moving in with your partner is a big step that will significantly impact your relationship. If you move in before you're ready, it may hurt your life and relationship. Make sure you're ready and doing it for the right reasons.

Thinking about living together should spark feelings of joy and excitement. It should be paired with a desire to deepen your relationship and commitment to your special someone. It should feel right. Uneasy feelings about the whole thing are a bad sign. Listen to your intuition and try to put good thought into it before deciding to move in with someone.
