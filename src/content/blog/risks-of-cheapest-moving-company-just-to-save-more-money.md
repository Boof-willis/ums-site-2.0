## Low Quality of Service, Unreliable Company

Risks of choosing the cheapest moving company to save money:

1. The quality of service may be lower than expected, and they may not handle your belongings with care. When it comes to your belongings, you can't put a price tag on peace of mind. Hiring a reliable mover is worth the investment and will ensure a smooth and stress-free move. If you choose the cheapest option without considering quality, there are risks that your belongings may be damaged or lost.

2. The company may not be reliable, and you could end up stranded on moving day. If the company is unprofessional or unreliable, there's a risk that they may not show up on the day of your move, leaving you stranded. This is why it's important to research a company thoroughly before selecting them as your moving partner.

3. Your belongings may not be covered by insurance. If you choose a company that is the cheapest option, there's a chance that they may not have adequate insurance to protect your belongings in case of damage or loss. Make sure to ask about insurance coverage before selecting a moving company, so you can be sure your belongings are protected.

4. There may be hidden fees that you're not aware of. Some moving companies lure customers in with low initial prices, only to hit them with unexpected charges later on. Ensure you understand all the fees involved before hiring a company so there are no unpleasant surprises.

5. The company may not be licensed or insured. If this is the case, you may be held responsible for any damage or loss during the move. Hence, always ask for proof of insurance and licensing before hiring a moving company.

What you should do:

There are many affordable and professional moving companies out there. The key is to conduct thorough research to find the right company for your needs. Make sure to read reviews, get quotes from multiple companies, and understand the fees involved before deciding. Check out our list of partners to get started.

Similarly, ask for referrals from friends or family who have recently moved. They can provide first-hand insights about their experiences and can recommend good companies to you. And finally, negotiate with moving companies to get the best price possible.

## No Proper Insurance to Protect Your Belongings

When it comes to moving, most people want to save as much money as possible. This often leads them to choose the cheapest moving company they can find. While this may seem like a good idea, in theory, there are a lot of risks associated with using a cheap moving company.

First, these companies might not provide you with the level of service you need. They may not have the proper equipment to move your belongings safely, or they may not have enough workers to do the job quickly. As a result, you could end up having to do a lot of the work yourself, which could end up costing you more in the long run.

Another risk associated with using a cheap moving company is that they may not have the proper insurance in place to protect your belongings. This means if something gets damaged during the move, you will not be compensated for it. This can be a very costly mistake, especially if you have valuable items that are damaged.

Finally, using a cheap moving company that is not reliable will only add to the stress of moving. You will have to deal with the hassle of trying to track down your belongings once they arrive at your new home, and you may even have to deal with the company trying to scam you out of more money. This wastes valuable time and money, which could have been used to settle into your new home.

But then again, this may not always be the case. There could be moving companies out there that offer good services at a very low price. However, you may have to look very hard to find them. You also have to be very careful when selecting a company, as there are many scams out there.

Just because a company is cheap, does not mean they are necessarily the best choice. It's a risky proposition, but it's worth looking into if you're on a tight budget. The best tip is to get multiple quotes from different companies before you make a final decision. Compare each company's services, and see which one seems like the better deal. Also, be sure to read reviews of the company online so that you can see what other customers have to say about their experience.

## Inexperienced or Untrustworthy Movers

The movers could be inexperienced and not equipped for the move. Cheap moving companies often have shoddy hiring practices - they may not run a complete background check on their movers, which may mean they are not appropriately vetted, insured, and/or licensed to work (i.e., their movers are not trustworthy).

Just because you are receiving a "cheap" up-front quote doesn't mean you are protected from hidden and/or add-on fees, which can add up quickly.

Many moving companies will start charging before your move has officially begun - they "start the clock" when their truck rolls off the lot (versus when they arrive at a customer's home/begin moving their belongings).

Early upfront quotes may not reflect the actual, total cost of the move. Some moving companies have lots of add-ons: gas and mileage fees, charges for furniture assembly and disassembly, additional fees for moving extra heavy/unwieldy items, and even fees for providing essential moving equipment (e.g., dolly, padding, etc)
