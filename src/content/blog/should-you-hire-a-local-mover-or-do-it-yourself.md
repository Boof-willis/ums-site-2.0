Moving can be a stressful experience, especially when you're moving locally. While local moves are generally faster and more affordable than long-distance moves, there are still important factors to consider before hiring a local moving company in Utah. Here are some tips to help you make the right decisions for your move.

## Should You Hire Local Movers or Do It Yourself?

Hiring local movers is the most convenient option for a local move but also the most expensive. Companies often charge by the hour for local moves, also taking into account travel time and gas fees. The cost for a local moving company might fall somewhere between $300- $1500. It all depends on the company, the distance, and the size of your move. The average local movers comes to about $25-$50 an hour per mover.

If you’re moving locally, you may decide you can handle doing it yourself and just recruit a few buddies to help. However, before you commit to such an undertaking, ask yourself these five questions:

- Are any of your family members or friends available to help you?
- Do you have a truck? Or have a friend who can lend you their vehicle for the move?
- Will you need help carrying heavy furniture and appliances?
- Are you moving fragile or extra valuable items?
- Do you require special moving services (i.e., pool table, piano, gym equipment)?

The answers to these questions will help you decide whether you need to hire a local mover or if this is something you can handle on your own.

## How to Find Local Movers

Hiring local movers involves researching to ensure you work with a professional moving company. Ideally, you want to work with a trustworthy local moving company well-known in the area. Here are some tips for finding reliable local moving companies in Utah that you can trust with your home possessions:

### 1. Ask friends, relatives, or neighbors for recommendations

Word-of-mouth recommendations can save you plenty of time in research. A great way to get the inside scoop on local moving companies is to talk to people who have used them. Ask your friends and family who they used on their move and what their experience was like. This will give you a great basis of information to build upon. If your friends had bad experiences with certain moving companies, stay away from them.

### 2. Read the company's reviews and testimonials before contacting them

Any reputable moving company in the Utah area will have an online presence with reviews on sites like Yelp, My Moving Reviews, and the Better Business Bureau. This is another great place to start. Don’t dare choose a company who has nothing but pages of negative reviews. Consider a company that has amazing online reviews from real customers.

### 3. Requesting moving cost estimates

Be wary of lowball estimates. If the cost seems too good to be true, then it probably is. Unfortunately, there is a lot of fraud out there. Ensure the moving company can provide a written quote before you agree to sign any contract with them. When hiring local movers, you must familiarize yourself with the average costs in your area to recognize common red flags that point to moving fraud.

The ultimate cost of hiring local movers will depend on the size and number of items they'll move. This is why many local moving companies will ask for the number of boxes and furniture pieces you expect to have before giving you an accurate quote. Make sure you also calculate how much space you need for your move. On average:

- Studio Apartment: two movers, three hours, for $240.
- 2-Bedroom Apartment: three movers, five hours, for $600.
- 3-Bedroom Apartment: four movers, seven hours, for $1,130.

This gives you an excellent basis to compare the rates and prices of moving companies. However, you must still add gas prices, time to and from the location, and other special charges. With the cost of moves increasing, it's essential to know precisely how much to budget.

## How to Save Money Moving Locally

Even though local moves are not as expensive as long-distance moves, you can still find ways to cut costs when moving locally. Like mentioned earlier, local transport prices are based on how long movers take to complete the job rather than on weight and distance. So, to help you save money when working with local movers, try to:

- Declutter and only bring what's necessary.
- Pack up things yourself, or at least try to have most boxes labeled, labeled, and ready to go when the movers arrive.
- Leave clothes, shoes, books, and other small items to move yourself. Bring these over in your car instead of having the movers carry them out.

## How Much to Tip Local Movers

Tipping local movers isn't something you are required or obligated to do. First, double-check your contract to verify whether or not they're already including gratuity in the total cost for the services. If tipping isn’t automatically included, tip based on your satisfaction with the service you received. You could forgo the tip if you had a lousy experience with your moving company or if they acted exceptionally unprofessional.

Conversely, you can show appreciation with a generous tip if they did a great job.

Generally, tipping $4 to $5 per hour per worker is considered a good tip. Another option is to tip 5% to 10% of the total move cost. Depending on the size of your move, you can expect to tip between $30 and $140 divided evenly among workers.
