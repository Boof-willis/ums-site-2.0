People move all the time. If you haven’t moved yourself yet, then you know someone who has. You may have heard all sorts of conflicting information about moving companies.

If it’s your turn to embark on the magical, stressful, and exhausting journey that is moving, you may be trying to decipher whether you should hire a moving company or not. Your friend really tried to sway you against them, but the idea of doing it yourself seems downright daunting.

We’re here to clear the air, debunk the myths, and help you make an informed decision. Here are five misconceptions about moving companies.

## 1. All Moving Companies Are the Same

Maybe someone has told you to stay far away from moving companies and gave you a laundry list of reasons why. Unfortunately, that person probably had a bad experience with ONE moving company and now has a bad taste for all moving companies.

Not all moving companies are created equal. Not all of them offer the same level of professionalism. Not all offer the same services. Companies vary in pricing, experience level, safety procedures, reliability, and reputation. Just like any industry, there’s good and bad, and it's important to put a little work into finding a good one to ensure a positive experience and outcome.

## 2. Moving Day Should Be on a Weekend

People usually pick a weekend for moving because it’s most convenient for them. No work, no school, it’s the perfect time. The only problem is, every other person moving is thinking the same thing. Moving companies are going to be busier on weekends, meaning you may have to compromise on the time slot you choose. This is especially true if it’s during summer, which is the prime moving season. In addition, you may have to pay a premium for services if you choose a heavily booked and busy day for the movers.

You could avoid being stuck with limited time slots and being slapped with premium charges by either booking with your moving company months in advance, or booking your moving date for a day in the middle of the week at an off-peak time such as morning or afternoon. Taking the day off of work may be worth it to move during the week and get the ideal time slot and undivided attention of your movers.

## 3. Moving By Yourself is More Cost-Effective

Most people tend to assume that DIY moves without professional movers will save you money. However, it’s important to consider that when you factor in the cost of specialized equipment and materials you need, it can add up to being just as expensive or more expensive than hiring a professional moving company. Not to mention the added time and energy it will take if you’re doing the heavy lifting yourself with a few buddies.

Full-service movers already have all the necessary equipment and materials, and they are proficient at handling every aspect of the packing, moving, and unpacking process. Your time is valuable, and professional movers ensure efficiency. They really do take a load off. Do the math to see which option would be more cost-effective and worth it for you.

## 4. There’s No Need for Labeling Your Boxes

Labeling boxes is a must. It will help the moving process move smoothly and efficiently. Organization is the fundamental key to a successful move. You know your items and home best, so label them according to which room you want them to be in. As you are labeling your boxes, make a mental note of where your labels are located on the boxes and what they look like. This will help you easily spot them once you arrive at your new destination.

Labeling can also help protect fragile or delicate items. A “fragile” label will tell movers to handle it with care and not to stack a box full of heavy books on top of your nice dishes. One option for labeling is using a color-coded system where you designate a certain color to each category of items.

## 5. Some Items Will Inevitably Break or Get Lost

Accidents do happen, but for reputable moving companies, they are rare. Professional movers who are experienced are trained in packing, unpacking and moving items to minimize damage and keep belongings secure. It’s true that some moving companies may have a track record of frequently lost or broken items, but there are plenty of Utah moving companies who take every precaution to avoid mishaps and treat your items as if they were their own. Our movers secure your belongings to ensure your things arrive undamaged.

Make sure your moving company uses protective padding and slides to prevent damage to your furniture. If you’re feeling extra anxious about your belongings being lost, stolen or damaged, make sure to talk to your movers about insurance coverage options to give you peace of mind.

## Move Forward With Someone You Trust

Don’t believe everything you hear about movers. All the misconceptions you’ve heard about moving companies are about the ones at the bottom of the barrel that probably have bad online reviews. Remember, not all moving companies are created equal. Not all have the same philosophies and dedication to their customers.

Doing some research when choosing a moving company will pay off. If you choose a company with positive online reviews, great communication, extensive experience, and the proper certifications, none of the above will be an issue.
