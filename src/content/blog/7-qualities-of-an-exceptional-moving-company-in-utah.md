Finding a moving company is like finding a boyfriend or girlfriend in this day and age. You've got to play the field, stalk them on social media, search the photo gallery, and look for any red flags before taking things to the next step. Technology is your friend when looking for a moving service, or a date.

An honest and reputable moving company will make your life exponentially easier. They'll take a huge burden off of you on moving day. They'll communicate well, treat you respectfully, and handle your belongings carefully.

Hiring the wrong moving company with dreadful customer service and organization skills is the perfect recipe for a panic attack. Things can go south quickly. It's up to you to weed out the bad ones and hire a great one. Cue the stalking skills. And if they don’t even have a website, forget it.

A move can be stressful, but choosing the right moving company will ease your overwhelm and take a load off your plate. Here are seven qualities to look for in a moving company.

### 1. They Have Lots of Experience

How long have they been in the moving game? A moving company that has assisted in dozens of moves will know how to move your stuff properly and safely. You can rest easy knowing that your special art piece, passed down from generations, is in good hands. And your antique kitchen table will stay upright. Make sure to find out about their experience level, including asking how much experience they have with your specific type of move. In some industries, it's OK to give someone brand new a chance to gain experience but be wary before trusting inexperienced movers.

### 2. They Have All the Right Resources

An experienced and reputable moving company will have all the resources necessary to tailor their services to their client's specific needs and requirements. Do they have all the right trucks, equipment, and storage options to ensure a smooth and successful transportation? Do they have all the proper licenses, documentation, and proof of insurance available to show their customers? Do they offer packing and storage services as well? Determine what you need from movers and make sure they have it.

### 3. They are Long-distance Movers

Extra brownie points for movers with experience in every type of move, including local, cross-country, and business. If they've done it before, they can do it for you. If you're embarking on a long-distance move, ensure your moving company has that kind of moving experience. Don't be the long-distance mover guinea pig.

### 4. They are Good Communicators

Hiring a moving company that takes forever to get a hold of and doesn't communicate well when you finally get them on the phone will take years off your life. Ask about their availability and get a feel for how they operate and prioritize communication. Bad communication is a huge red flag.

### 5. They are Reliable

Check their online reviews. Talk to a friend who used them for their move. Find out how reliable they are. Reliability is vital for any relationship, even your relationship with your movers. Doing your research to determine how trustworthy a moving company is will be worth it. It will give you so much peace of mind. Vet your moving company before hiring them and ask around about how reliable and honest they are.

### 6. They are Transparent

There is nothing more frustrating than hidden fees and unexpected receipts. Transparency is everything. Trustworthy movers will offer free estimates and give you a clear picture of how they calculate their prices. Get quotes from a few local movers, and if one quote seems way too good to be true, it probably is. Use your judgment and ensure your moving company is honest and straightforward about everything.

### 7. They are Efficient

After a week of never-ending preparation tasks for moving day, you will appreciate efficient movers more than you know, especially if you're hiring full-service movers who pack and move your stuff. The best moving companies know how to get a job done as fast and efficiently as possible with no damage and no issues. Efficiency should be a given if you're paying for a moving company. Looking at online reviews will help determine how efficient a moving company is.
