## Living in Orem, Utah

Orem, Utah, is home to a variety of attractions for visitors and locals alike. Culture is right around the corner at Hale Center Theatre, which offers live performances of plays, musicals, and much more throughout the year. There’s something on the playbill here to entertain any age group.

When you’re ready to get outdoors, Orem does not disappoint. Mount Timpanogos Park provides incredible views of the Wasatch Mountain Range with its 8,500-foot elevation. You can take a scenic hike, go camping, visit the dog park with your 4-legged friends, or simply picnic in the park.

Alternatively, Nielsen's Grove Park is the perfect spot for a day out with friends and family. This 21-acre park boasts a duck pond, playgrounds, basketball courts, and 25,000 tulips in spring that nod to its Danish heritage.

If you love to learn, we’re close to Provo’s Brigham Young University and even closer to Utah Valley University here in Orem. UVU is known for its affordability and excellent moral climate and for being one of the safest colleges in the country. It also boasts an excellent student-teacher ratio, with just 23 students per class on average.
