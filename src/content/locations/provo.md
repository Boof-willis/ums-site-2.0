## Living in Provo, Utah

Once you’re in your new Provo home, the stresses of your move will soon be forgotten. Provo is beautiful, affordable, and fun, and has an exciting social scene with something for everyone. From food festivals to live music, there’s always something fun happening in this vibrant Utah city.

Rock Canyon Park in Provo, Utah, is a popular outdoor destination for locals and tourists alike. Located near the Provo River and with breathtaking views of the Wasatch Mountains, Rock Canyon Park has something to offer everyone. Bird watching, hiking, nature photography opportunities, base jumping, snowboarding, and horseback riding are all on offer here.

The Covey Center for the Arts in Provo, Utah is a premier destination for performing arts and education. The venue has been a vital part of the local community since its opening in 1984, providing a home to music, dance, theater, art galleries, lectures, and more. The Covey Center boasts two large theatres to accommodate all types of events. The venue’s many classrooms are the site of instruction in many different disciplines, from comedy improvisation to vocal performance and dance.

And, of course, take advantage of the many offerings at Brigham Young University, one of the highest-ranked schools in the country bustling with motivated, dedicated students. From music and dance performances by BYU’s Young Ambassadors or International Folk Dance Ensemble to football games in Lavell Edwards Stadium, there’s plenty to keep you engaged all year round.
